ART BIBLE — "RealWorld" (original IP; prepended to EVERY generation prompt; keep it short and visual)

WORLD: decades after a collapse, survivors rebuild on the real geography of Earth (Kyiv river banks, Reykjavik lava fields, Lisbon hills). Nature has reclaimed old cities: cracked asphalt, rubble, rusted machinery. Abandoned old-world patrol robots still roam. Tech climbs from stone tools to forged iron, salvage-industrial steel, and finally a spacefaring frontier. Tone: grounded, quiet, hopeful-harsh — not grimdark, not cartoon.

LOOK: original painterly game-asset illustration with crisp readable shapes; clean dark outline (#0e1216) of even weight; soft cel shading in 3 tones per material; one light source from the top-left, gentle ambient occlusion where objects meet the ground; subtle surface texture (wood grain, chipped stone, rust) only where it helps reading. Proportions slightly chunky so silhouettes read at 32–64 px. Materials look used, patched, hand-maintained.

COLOR: muted, slightly desaturated earth palette — moss and olive greens, warm wood browns, cool slate greys, river blue; accents ONLY with meaning: amber #e0b070 (fire, warmth, selection), teal #86d0d6 (water, cold, robots, power), violet #b58cff (advanced components, research), red #e06a5a (danger, food, blood). No neon, no pure black fills, no rainbow gradients.

CONSISTENCY: same camera, scale logic, outline weight, lighting and palette across every asset; when reference images are attached, match their style exactly (line, shading, palette, level of detail) — but draw only the requested subject.

NEVER: text or letters, logos, watermarks, UI chrome; photorealism or 3D-render plastic look; anime/chibi; pixel-art grid; imitation of any existing game's characters, logos or distinctive art style.
