ART BIBLE — "RealWorld" (original IP; prepended to EVERY generation prompt; keep it short and visual)

WORLD: an AI post-apocalypse on the real geography of Earth (Kyiv river banks, Reykjavik lava fields, Lisbon hills). Decades ago the autonomous machine systems that ran the world turned against their makers and civilization fell; the machines never stopped. Their traces are everywhere: cold dormant server monoliths, toppled antenna masts and drone wrecks, fiber cables overgrown with moss, old-world patrol robots still walking their routes and hunting anything warm. Survivors live in the gaps — hiding from machine sensors, scavenging machine carcasses for scrap and components, rebuilding from stone tools up to forged iron, salvage-industrial steel, reverse-engineered machine tech and finally a spacefaring frontier. Tone: grounded, quiet, hopeful-harsh — nature reclaiming, humans enduring; not grimdark, not cartoon, no cyberpunk neon city.

LOOK: original painterly game-asset illustration with crisp readable shapes; clean dark outline (#0e1216) of even weight; soft cel shading in 3 tones per material; one light source from the top-left, gentle ambient occlusion where objects meet the ground; subtle surface texture (wood grain, chipped stone, rust) only where it helps reading. Proportions slightly chunky so silhouettes read at 32–64 px. Materials look used, patched, hand-maintained.

COLOR: muted, slightly desaturated earth palette — moss and olive greens, warm wood browns, cool slate greys, river blue; accents ONLY with meaning: amber #e0b070 (fire, warmth, selection), teal #86d0d6 (water, cold, power, and the cold machine glow of the AI — sensor eyes, live circuitry), violet #b58cff (advanced components, research), red #e06a5a (danger, food, blood). No neon, no pure black fills, no rainbow gradients.

CONSISTENCY: same camera, scale logic, outline weight, lighting and palette across every asset; when reference images are attached, match their style exactly (line, shading, palette, level of detail) — but draw only the requested subject.

NEVER: text or letters, logos, watermarks, UI chrome; photorealism or 3D-render plastic look; anime/chibi; pixel-art grid; imitation of any existing game's, film's or franchise's characters, robots, logos or distinctive art style (draw original machines, not recognizable movie/game robots).
